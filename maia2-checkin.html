MAIA-2 Body-Awareness Check-in

A short self-portrait of how you relate to your body's inner signals — across eight facets. There are no good or bad scores. It's a mirror you can return to.

What this is — and isn't. This measures interoceptive sensibility (how attuned you feel, by self-report). That is a different thing from interoceptive accuracy (how well you'd actually track a body signal on a test) — and the two often diverge. Feeling attuned isn't the same as being accurate. Hold the result as reflection, not assessment.
Items follow the MAIA-2 (Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness, version 2; Mehling et al., 2018), freely available under Creative Commons. Wording here is reproduced to the editors' best fidelity — verify against the official instrument before any research or clinical use. Not a medical test; no diagnosis is implied.
