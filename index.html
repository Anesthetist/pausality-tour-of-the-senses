A Tour of the Senses

Three small instruments for the time we spend together. Open them on your own machine — they run offline and keep everything to themselves.

The felt present, the sense of self, and the weather of emotion are all built from the body's signals — and those signals are constructed, not simply read. So the leverage point of a life is learning to calibrate them. That's the whole practice: mind the gap, and make the present.
